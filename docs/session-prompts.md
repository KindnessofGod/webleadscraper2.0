# Copy-paste prompts for the other Claude sessions

Three prompts. Paste each into the session that owns that work.
Do prompt 1 and 2 before the next outreach campaign goes out.

---

## Prompt 1 — for EACH of the five demo-site sessions

> This site is a concept build for my agency portfolio — there is no real business
> behind it. Right now it presents itself as a real operating company, which will
> break my credibility the moment a prospect searches the name and finds nothing.
> I need it to stay just as impressive visually while no longer making claims that
> can be checked and disproved. Make these changes and nothing else:
>
> 1. **Remove every fabricated testimonial or review.** Delete any quote attributed
>    to a named person, and delete any text attributing reviews to Google Reviews or
>    any other platform. If the layout needs the section, replace the content with a
>    clearly labelled `Sample testimonial` placeholder — but deleting the section is
>    preferred.
> 2. **Replace the contact details with obvious placeholders.** Any street address,
>    phone number or email that looks real must go — those belong to actual people.
>    Use something unmistakably illustrative (e.g. `Address shown for demonstration`,
>    `+234 000 000 0000`) and remove `tel:` / `mailto:` links so nothing dials out.
> 3. **Remove any specific factual claim that could be checked** — founding years,
>    staff counts, awards, certifications, client numbers, "as featured in".
> 4. **Check the invented business name against real businesses.** Search the name
>    plus "Lagos" and "Nigeria". If a real company already uses it, rename the demo.
>    Tell me if you had to rename anything.
> 5. **Add one quiet line in the footer**, small, muted, not a banner:
>    `Sample build — {NICHE}. Designed by iKAN Growth Enterprise.`
>    Link "iKAN Growth Enterprise" to https://ikan-growth-redesign.vercel.app/
> 6. **Do not change the design, layout, typography, colours, imagery or copy tone.**
>    The visual quality is the entire point of the portfolio piece — it must look
>    exactly as good afterwards.
>
> Then redeploy and give me the live URL.

Substitute `{NICHE}` per site:

| Site | `{NICHE}` |
|---|---|

## Prompt 2 — for the PDF session

> Page 4 of the company profile is headed "Here's what we've built" and describes
> the five sites as "A selection of websites we've built across different business
> categories". Every reader takes those as past clients. They are not — they are
> concept builds I designed to demonstrate range, and there is no real business
> behind any of them. A prospect who searches "Aureline Dental House" finds nothing,
> and I go from looking legitimate to looking fake.
>
> Rewrite page 4 so it presents them honestly as concept work, without sounding
> apologetic or weakening the page. Concept and spec work is normal agency practice,
> so state it with confidence. Direction:
>
> - Heading: something in the spirit of "Concept builds" or "Designed to show what's
>   possible" rather than "Here's what we've built".
> - Body: make clear these were designed by us to demonstrate what a site in each
>   category can look like — that they are our own work start to finish, not client
>   projects. Frame that as a strength: every pixel is ours.
> - Add one line inviting the natural next step: we can build a concept for *their*
>   business the same way.
> - Keep the five thumbnails, category labels, names and URLs exactly as they are.
> - Keep the existing visual design, grid and typography unchanged.
> - Change nothing on pages 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 or 7.
>
> Then export the updated PDF.

## Prompt 3 — addendum for the outreach automation session

Use alongside `whatsapp-outreach-brief.md`.

> Change the campaign structure to run **one niche at a time** instead of a mixed
> list. For each campaign, every prospect is in the same category, and the proof
> link we send is the concept build for that category:
>
> | Niche | Link |

> | Dental & healthcare | https://dental-bay-ten.vercel.app/ |

>
> Rules:
> - Send the **link**, not the PDF. The PDF goes only when asked, or once the
>   prospect has clearly engaged.
> - Describe the link honestly — it is a site we designed to show what's possible
>   in their category, not a past client. Never imply we built it for a real
>   business. Example: *"Here's one we designed for a Lagos dental practice —
>   this is the standard we build to: {link}"*
> - Never send a niche's link to a prospect outside that niche.
> - A custom preview of the prospect's own site is offered **only** after a human
>   reply from a qualified decision-maker — it is built by hand and we can produce
>   a few per week, not per day.

## Why these changes

From the 250-conversation export:

- **Sewgull 1172** asked *"How big are you?"* and then said *"U beta go and grow
  urself first."* A finished site in their own category answers that in one tap.
- **Roots N Tips** said *"is there anyone that cannot build their own websites …
  I'm tired of people trying to sell things we all can do in less than 30 minutes."*
  A polished niche build is the only reply that lands.
- The pitch went out byte-identical 63 times, always citing *"dental, restaurants,
  real estate"* — to spas, salons, car dealers and guest houses. Niche-by-niche
  campaigns remove that mismatch by construction.
